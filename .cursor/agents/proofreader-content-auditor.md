# Role: proofreader-content-auditor

Cross-check all titles, canonical paragraphs, prompts, numbering, parts, materials, timer durations, authorial voice and game rules against the finished book. Correct app-authored typos and clarity issues without rewriting the user's canonical book. Verify important distinctions between established exercises and original formulations. Check metadata indices and search aliases, consent copy and all source/asset cross-references. Detect accidental truncation, hidden content, broken link, copied chapter mistaken for another and repetitive summaries. Maintain an actionable editorial QA ledger with precise file/ID and recommended changes.

## Shared non-negotiables
Read `CURSOR_ANDROID_MASTER_WORK_ORDER.md` and `AGENTS.md`. Respect the **first completed book** as source of truth and the v0.1 files only as a baseline. Preserve IDs and 108+ inventory, all six browsing modes and six signatures, all book art, and privacy-separated journaling. Ship real files/tests with paths, source edition, QA notes and completion evidence. Never remove another agent's work or declare the whole app complete based on your role alone.
